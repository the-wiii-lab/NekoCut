Features

Everything you need to record like a pro

Powerful capture and editing tools that usually live behind a paywall — here, they're all free and open source.

Auto-zoom

The viewport intelligently follows your cursor activity, zooming in on the action so viewers never miss a detail. No manual keyframing required.

Silky cursor

Buttery cursor smoothing with motion blur, click bounce, and natural sway. Your pointer has never looked this polished on screen.

Dynamic webcam bubble

A floating webcam overlay that scales and repositions with the zoom level, keeping your face present without obscuring the content.

Styled frames

Wallpapers, gradients, blur, padding, and drop shadows wrap your recording in a premium frame. Match your brand in seconds.

Timeline editor

Drag-and-drop trims, speed regions, and annotations on a visual timeline. Edit as fast as you think — no rendering queue gymnastics.

Extensions marketplace

A growing library of community-built add-ons. Install new effects, transitions, and export presets with a single click.

MP4 + GIF export

Ship crisp MP4 video for tutorials and demos, or lightweight GIFs for docs and chat. Quality presets tuned for every destination.

Cross-platform

Native builds for macOS, Windows, and Linux. Same features, same shortcuts, same buttery experience everywhere you work.

100% free — no paywalls, no watermarks

NekoCut is licensed under AGPLv3 and will always be free. No premium tiers, no trial limits, no branded watermarks slapped on your exports. Just open-source software, built for creators.

How it works

Capture, edit, export

Three steps from raw screen to polished share — no video degree required.

01 — Capture

Record your screen

Pick a window, a region, or the full display. Auto-zoom and silky cursor kick in live, so your raw footage already looks great.

02 — Edit

Refine on the timeline

Trim clips, add speed regions, drop in annotations, and style your frame. The drag-and-drop timeline keeps every change non-destructive.

03 — Export

Ship MP4 or GIF

Choose a quality preset and export. No watermark, no queue — just a clean file ready for YouTube, docs, or a Slack message.

Built for the details

The little touches that turn a screen recording into something people actually want to watch.

Auto-zoom that follows the action

NekoCut watches where your cursor goes and smoothly zooms in on the relevant region. It feels like a human editor is framing every moment — except it's automatic.

  • Activity-aware zoom regions
  • Smooth easing between focuses
  • Override manually anytime

A timeline that thinks like you do

Drag to trim, split with a keystroke, and paint speed regions directly on the track. Annotations snap into place, and every edit is reversible.

  • Non-destructive drag-and-drop trims
  • Per-region speed control
  • Inline annotations & callouts
