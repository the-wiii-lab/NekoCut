Features

Everything you need to
make recordings that shine

Auto-zoom & silky cursor effects

NekoCut automatically tracks your cursor and zooms into what matters. Smooth spring physics make every click feel intentional — no more jittery mouse movements distracting from your demo.

  • Automatic click detection with spring-eased zoom
  • Smooth cursor trail and click ripple effects
  • Configurable zoom factor and smoothing

Webcam bubble overlay

Add a personal touch with a floating webcam bubble. Position it anywhere, round it, or shape it — your viewers see your face without losing screen context.

  • Drag-to-position anywhere on the canvas
  • Round, rounded, or square crop shapes
  • Resize and adjust on the fly

Drag-and-drop timeline editor

A real timeline — not just a trim bar. Stack clips, split segments, reposition layers, and fine-tune timing visually. What you see is exactly what you export.

  • Multi-track timeline with visual layering
  • Split, trim, and reorder clips by dragging
  • Frame-accurate preview before export
More to love

Built for every workflow

System & mic audio

Capture computer audio and microphone simultaneously, with independent level control on each track.

Export to MP4, WebM & GIF

Export in the format you need. Choose resolution, frame rate, and quality to balance file size and clarity.

Import media

Drop in existing video, images, or audio files to layer them alongside your recording on the timeline.

Loop recording

Record continuously and keep only the last segment you need — perfect for capturing hard-to-reproduce moments.

Save & resume projects

Your timeline is saved as a project file. Close NekoCut, come back tomorrow, and pick up right where you left off.

Cross-platform & free

Runs natively on macOS, Windows, and Linux. No watermarks, no subscriptions, no account required. Ever.
